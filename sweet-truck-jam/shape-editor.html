Central Loop Shape Editor

Draw the centreline of the central sweet conveyor. Mix freehand curves with snapped straight sections, then copy the shape data back into ChatGPT.

Mode: Free Draw 0 points Open path
Free Draw: drag your finger to draw curves naturally. Snap Draw: tap/drag from the last point to create neat straight segments. The yellow preview shows the snapped result before you release. You can switch between modes at any time. Close Loop joins the final point back to the first. When you are happy, tap Finish + Copy and paste the copied data into this chat.
